Welcome

We're Antheloop, creators of Duck 2048 — a charming puzzle game where cute ducks challenge your strategic thinking. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information with complete transparency.

💡 Our Philosophy: We believe privacy should be simple. We don't collect personal information like your name, email, or location. What we do collect is minimal and only used to keep the game running smoothly.

What We Never Collect

Duck 2048 is privacy-first by design. Here's what you'll never see us asking for:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Precise location or GPS coordinates
  • Device contacts, calendar, or media files
  • Personal account credentials or passwords
  • Data that identifies you personally in-game
  • Biometric information
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)
✓ Verified: We've structured Duck 2048 to be completely anonymous. You can play without signing up or sharing any personal info.

Information We Automatically Receive

📱 Your Advertising ID

To show relevant ads, we use your device's Advertising ID — a unique, non-personal token assigned by your device's operating system. Think of it as a random number, not a personal identifier.

How we use it:

  • Display ads that match your interests
  • Measure how well our ads perform
  • Prevent fraud and duplicate advertising
  • Comply with advertising industry standards
🎮 Want to Opt Out? You're in complete control. To stop seeing personalized ads:
  1. Open Settings → Google → Manage Your Google Account
  2. Go to Data & Privacy section
  3. Find Ad Personalization and toggle it off

🔗 Ad Networks & Partners

We partner with third-party ad networks to show you ads. These networks may collect limited data in compliance with their privacy policies:

  • Device type and Android OS version
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks)
  • General region/country (never precise location)
  • Anonymous usage statistics

Learn more about how Google protects your privacy.

📊 Technical Device Data

To keep Duck 2048 running smoothly across different devices, we collect minimal technical data:

  • Your device model and manufacturer
  • Android operating system version
  • Duck 2048 app version
  • Crash reports and error logs (for debugging)
  • Anonymous gameplay metrics (session length, features used)

How We Use Your Data

✨ Better Advertising

Your Advertising ID helps us show ads that are actually relevant to you, not random spam. It also helps us detect and prevent advertising fraud across the ecosystem.

🎮 Improving the Game

Technical data helps us make Duck 2048 better:

  • Performance: Optimize the game for your device
  • Bug Fixes: Identify and fix crashes
  • Features: Understand which features players enjoy
  • Stability: Ensure the game runs smoothly

⚖️ Legal Obligations

We use data to comply with laws, enforce our terms of service, and protect against fraud and abuse.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the technical information we receive. Our approach includes encryption, secure transmission, and limited access controls.

⚠️ Important Note: Since we don't collect personal data, the risk of identity theft or privacy violation is extremely low. The data we do collect is minimally sensitive.

No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take every reasonable precaution to safeguard your data.

Children's Privacy

Duck 2048 is designed to be family-friendly for all ages. We strictly comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act in the US) and Google Play's Families Policy.

  • ✓ We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • ✓ No account registration or sign-up required to play
  • ✓ We never request personal data from any user
  • ✓ All advertising follows family-friendly guidelines

For Parents & Guardians: You can enable parental controls in Google Play Settings and adjust advertising preferences in your device settings at any time.

Your Privacy Rights

🎯 Control Your Ads

  • Opt out of personalized advertising in device settings
  • Reset your Advertising ID at any time
  • Review and adjust your Google ad preferences

🔐 App Permissions

You can review which permissions Duck 2048 has requested and grant or deny them in your device settings. We keep our permission requests to a minimum.

📞 Contact & Support

Have questions about your privacy or our practices? Contact us anytime — we're happy to help. We'll respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.

Legal Compliance

🇪🇺 GDPR (European Users)

If you're located in the European Union, here's how we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • We don't collect GDPR-protected personal data
  • Your Advertising ID may be regulated as personal data — you control it through Google
  • You have the right to file complaints with your local data protection authority
  • We maintain data processing agreements with third parties

🇺🇸 CCPA/CPRA (California Users)

California residents have strong privacy rights. Here's our compliance:

  • We do not "sell" personal information as defined by CCPA
  • We do not "share" personal information for behavioral advertising
  • Your Advertising ID is protected under CPRA regulations
  • Contact us to exercise your rights

📋 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We'll notify you of material changes by updating the date on this page. By continuing to use Duck 2048, you agree to the updated policy.
